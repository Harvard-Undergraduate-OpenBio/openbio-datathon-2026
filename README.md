# OpenBio 2026 Datathon

**Two tracks on women's health: preeclampsia and cardiovascular disease**

[![OpenBio](https://img.shields.io/badge/OPENBIO-OPENBIOLAB.ORG-0fe995?style=flat-square)](https://www.openbiolab.org/)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-13deba?style=flat-square)](LICENSE)

Welcome to the Harvard Undergraduate OpenBio 2026 Datathon. You do not need a biology or statistics background to take part. Each track's guide explains ideas as they come up.

## Choose a Track

| Track | The challenge | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| **Track 1: Maternal-Fetal Cell Communication in Preeclampsia** | Compare gene activity in placentas from pregnancies with and without preeclampsia, then ask which placental cell types might explain the differences | [track-01/README.md](track-01/README.md) |
| **Track 2: Cardiovascular Health in Women** | Use patient records and global statistics to ask who is at risk of heart disease and how that risk differs for women | [track-02/README.md](track-02/README.md) |

## Start Here

Both tracks follow the same steps. Work through the pages in your track's folder in order.

| Step | Page | What you will do | Track 1 | Track 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Track overview | Learn what the challenge is about | [Open](track-01/README.md) | [Open](track-02/README.md) |
| 1 | Setup | Get the software and data ready | [Open](track-01/1-setup.md) | [Open](track-02/1-setup.md) |
| 2 | Understand the data | Learn what each file contains | [Open](track-01/2-the-data.md) | [Open](track-02/2-the-data.md) |
| 3 | Analysis workflow | Follow seven steps from raw data to a finding | [Open](track-01/3-workflow.md) | [Open](track-02/3-workflow.md) |
| 4 | Submit | Package your work for judging | [Open](track-01/4-submit.md) | [Open](track-02/4-submit.md) |

Keep your track's glossary open while you work ([Track 1](track-01/glossary.md), [Track 2](track-02/glossary.md)), and pick a research question from its starter questions ([Track 1](track-01/starter-questions.md), [Track 2](track-02/starter-questions.md)).
