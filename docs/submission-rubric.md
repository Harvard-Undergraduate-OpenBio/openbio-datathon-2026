# Submission Rubric

## Track 1: Computational Biology

| Criterion | Weight | What Judges Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Biological question and rationale | 20% | A focused maternal–fetal communication question with clear relevance to preeclampsia |
| Data rigor and reproducibility | 25% | Correct use of metadata, code, appropriate unit of inference, transparent preprocessing |
| Analytical quality | 25% | Sensible model/tool choices, controls, interpretation of uncertainty |
| Hypothesis quality | 20% | Testable, biologically plausible, not overstated from observational inference |
| Communication and visual clarity | 10% | Clear figures, explanation, and accessible final presentation |

## Track 2: Global Health

| Criterion | Weight | What Judges Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem framing | 20% | Clear identification of health-system context and intervention question |
| Data rigor and reproducibility | 25% | Correct use of data, appropriate aggregation, transparent methods |
| Analytical quality | 25% | Appropriate methods, context identification, intervention analysis |
| Recommendation quality | 20% | Practical, context-appropriate, evidence-based intervention proposals |
| Communication and visual clarity | 10% | Clear figures, explanation, and accessible final presentation |

## Submission Requirements

Each team must submit:

1. One reproducible notebook or script (committed to the repository)
2. 2–4 figures (as PNG or PDF)
3. A brief written interpretation (1–2 pages)
4. One completed hypothesis card (Track 1) or intervention proposal (Track 2)
