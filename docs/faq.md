# FAQ

## General

**Who can participate?**
The datathon is open to undergraduate students at Harvard and affiliated institutions. Teams of two to four are recommended.

**Do I need prior bioinformatics experience?**
Track 1 is designed to be accessible to beginners with some programming background. The data arrive prepared and starter notebooks are provided. Advanced teams can take on the optional single-cell extension.

**What should I bring?**
A laptop with Python or R installed. We provide environment files for easy setup.

## Track 1

**Do I need to download raw data?**
No. Everything is in the prepared package. Follow the [download guide](../data/track-01/README.md).

**Which comparison should we run?**
That is your choice. Pick a question from the [starter questions](track-1-computational-biology/research-questions.md) and justify your comparison.

**Which methods or tools should we use?**
Any, as long as you document them. The starter notebooks cover loading and checking the data only.

**Is the single-cell extension required?**
No. The bulk core is required for every team. The gene browser is required for at least three genes.

**Can I use additional datasets?**
Yes, if they are public and cited. Start with the resources in the [data catalog](track-1-computational-biology/data-catalog.md).

## Submissions

**What format should my submission be?**
A reproducible notebook or script, two to four figures, a brief written interpretation and a completed hypothesis card. See the [templates](../templates/) folder.

**How are submissions judged?**
See the [submission rubric](submission-rubric.md) for scoring criteria.
