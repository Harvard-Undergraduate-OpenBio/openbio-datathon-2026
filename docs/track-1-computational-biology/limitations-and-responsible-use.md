# Limitations and Responsible Use

## What This Data Can and Cannot Show

- Bulk RNA-seq averages gene activity across a tissue sample. A difference can come from a change in cell mix rather than a change within cells.
- Placental tissue is collected at delivery. It reflects late pregnancy, not the earlier events that may start preeclampsia.
- Delivery timing differs between groups. Gestational age and control type can explain part of any difference.
- Expression patterns can suggest signaling between cells. They do not show that signaling happened or that it causes disease.

## Responsible Reporting

- Treat the donor as the unit of replication.
- Keep missing values missing and report flagged values rather than correcting them.
- Separate what you observed from the mechanism you propose.
- Prefer "consistent with" or "suggests" over "shows" or "proves."
- Propose a concrete follow-up that could test your hypothesis.
- Cite every data source.
