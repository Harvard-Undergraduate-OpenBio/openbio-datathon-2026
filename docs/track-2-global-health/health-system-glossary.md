# Health-System Glossary

## Care Continuum

- **Antenatal care (ANC)** — Routine health check-ups during pregnancy to monitor maternal and fetal health. WHO recommends at least 8 contacts during pregnancy.
- **Screening** — Systematic testing for risk factors or disease; in PE, primarily blood pressure measurement and urine protein testing.
- **Referral** — Transfer of a patient from one level of care to a higher-level facility for specialized management.
- **Facility readiness** — The extent to which a health facility has the staff, equipment, supplies, and medications needed to provide a service.
- **Stockout** — Absence of essential supplies or medications at a health facility.
- **Emergency obstetric care (EmOC)** — Medical interventions for managing obstetric complications, including PE/eclampsia. Includes "basic" (BEmOC) and "comprehensive" (CEmOC) levels.

## Treatments

- **Magnesium sulfate (MgSO4)** — The standard medication for preventing and treating eclamptic seizures in PE.
- **Antihypertensive treatment** — Medications to control high blood pressure in PE (e.g., labetalol, nifedipine, methyldopa).

## Frameworks

- **Three Delays model** — A framework for analyzing maternal mortality: (1) delay in deciding to seek care, (2) delay in reaching care, (3) delay in receiving adequate care.
- **Care continuum** — The sequence of steps from prevention through screening, diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up.

## System Concepts

- **Transport delay** — Time lost traveling to a health facility; a major contributor to maternal mortality in low-resource settings.
- **Implementation feasibility** — Whether a proposed intervention can realistically be delivered in a given health-system context.
- **Equity** — Fair distribution of health resources and outcomes across populations.
- **Context-specific prioritization** — Recognizing that the most important intervention point differs by health-system context (e.g., urban vs. rural, high vs. low resource).
