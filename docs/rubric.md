# Submission Rubric

Both tracks hand in the same four deliverables and are scored out of 100%. Each track has its own criteria.

## Track 1: Computational Biology

| Criterion | Weight | What Judges Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Biological question and rationale | 20% | A focused placental question with clear relevance to preeclampsia |
| Data rigor and reproducibility | 25% | Correct use of metadata, donor as the unit of replication, reproducible code |
| Analytical quality | 25% | Justified comparisons and models, attention to confounders such as delivery timing |
| Hypothesis quality | 20% | Testable, biologically plausible and not overstated |
| Communication and visual clarity | 10% | Clear graphs, an accessible article and presentation |

## Track 2: Cardiovascular Health in Women

| Criterion | Weight | What Judges Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Research question and rationale | 20% | A focused question with clear relevance to women's cardiovascular health |
| Data rigor and reproducibility | 25% | Documented cleaning rules with the number of records each one affected, reproducible code |
| Analytical quality | 25% | A justified method, effect sizes with confidence intervals, checks on the model |
| Responsible interpretation | 20% | Claims that respect the [ground rules](../track-02/README.md#ground-rules), with limitations stated and nothing overstated |
| Communication and visual clarity | 10% | Clear graphs, an accessible article and presentation |

Judges look for honest, careful work over dramatic results.

## Submission Requirements

Each team in either track must submit:

1. Reproducible code that runs top to bottom, with a README
2. A recorded presentation of five minutes or less, uploaded to YouTube as an unlisted video
3. A science communication article that follows the [article guide](article-guide.md), with at least four graphs
4. One headline visual showing the team's most important finding

Each track adds its own working documents:

- **Track 1:** a completed analysis plan and one completed hypothesis card
- **Track 2:** a cleaning log listing each rule and the number of records affected

See [How to Submit](../submissions/README.md) for the folder layout, the two ways to submit and the deadline. Details for each deliverable are on the submit pages ([Track 1](../track-01/4-submit.md), [Track 2](../track-02/4-submit.md)).
