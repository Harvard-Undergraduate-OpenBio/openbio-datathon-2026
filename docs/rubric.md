# Submission Rubric

## Track 1: Computational Biology

| Criterion | Weight | What Judges Assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Biological question and rationale | 20% | A focused placental question with clear relevance to preeclampsia |
| Data rigor and reproducibility | 25% | Correct use of metadata, donor as the unit of replication, reproducible code |
| Analytical quality | 25% | Justified comparisons and models, attention to confounders such as delivery timing |
| Hypothesis quality | 20% | Testable, biologically plausible and not overstated |
| Communication and visual clarity | 10% | Clear figures and an accessible final presentation |

## Track 1 Submission Requirements

Each Track 1 team must submit:

1. One reproducible notebook or script (committed to the repository)
2. Two to four figures (as PNG or PDF)
3. A brief written interpretation (1-2 pages)
4. One completed hypothesis card

## Track 2: Cardiovascular Health in Women

Judging criteria for Track 2 will be announced. Judges will look for honest, careful work over dramatic results. See [Track 2: Submit](../track-02/4-submit.md) for the four deliverables.
