# OpenBio 2026 Datathon — Challenge Overview

## Summary

The OpenBio 2026 Datathon is a two-track event organized by Harvard Undergraduate OpenBio. Participants choose one of two tracks and work in teams to analyze publicly available biomedical or health-systems data.

## Track 1: Computational Biology

**Research Question:** Which ligand–receptor communication programs between fetal trophoblast and maternal decidual/immune cells are altered in preeclampsia, and which altered interactions yield the strongest testable hypotheses about failed placentation, immune tolerance, vascular remodeling, or maternal systemic immune activation?

Teams use publicly available single-cell RNA-seq datasets from preeclamptic and healthy placental tissue to infer differential cell–cell communication patterns.

- [Track 1 Overview](track-1-computational-biology/overview.md)
- [Track 1 Data Catalog](track-1-computational-biology/data-catalog.md)
- [Track 1 Research Questions](track-1-computational-biology/research-questions.md)

## Track 2: Global Health

**Research Question:** Which interactions between patients, facilities, screening systems, treatment resources, and referral networks are failing in preeclampsia care, and which health-system intervention points appear most important?

Teams use DHS Program data and related health-system resources to identify distinct health-system contexts and propose context-appropriate interventions.

- [Track 2 Overview](track-2-global-health/overview.md)

## Who Can Participate

The datathon is open to undergraduate students at Harvard and affiliated institutions. Teams of 2–4 are recommended. Mixed-experience teams are encouraged.

## Getting Started

1. Read the [challenge overview](challenge-overview.md) and select your track.
2. Review the [data catalog](track-1-computational-biology/data-catalog.md) for your track.
3. Set up your environment using the [environment files](../environment/).
4. Work through the starter notebooks in [`notebooks/`](../notebooks/).
5. Prepare your submission using the [templates](../templates/).
