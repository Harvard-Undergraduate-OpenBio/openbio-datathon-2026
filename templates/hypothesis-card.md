# Hypothesis Card

## Title

## Clinical/Biological Problem

What clinical or biological problem does this hypothesis address?

## Dataset(s) and Relevant Cells

Which datasets and cell types are involved?

## Observed Result

What did your analysis show? Be specific about the genes and cell types.

## Proposed Mechanism

What biological mechanism do you hypothesize explains the observed result?

## Why the Result Is Biologically Plausible

Connect to known biology. Cite relevant literature.

## Alternative Explanations/Confounders

What else could explain this result? What confounders have you considered?

## Proposed Validation Experiment or Follow-up Analysis

How would you test this hypothesis experimentally or computationally?

## Potential Intervention Relevance

Does this suggest a possible therapeutic or diagnostic target?

## Confidence Level and Limitations

State your confidence level (low/medium/high) and explain why. Acknowledge what expression data cannot establish.
