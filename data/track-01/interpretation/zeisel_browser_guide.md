# Zeisel/Admati Gene Browser Guide

[![Browser](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tool-Zeisel_Browser-0fe995?style=flat-square)](https://storage.googleapis.com/www_zeisellab/PE_web/pe_web_test_v1.html)
[![Marker Matrix](https://img.shields.io/badge/Data-Marker_Matrix-13deba?style=flat-square)](track01_marker_matrix.csv)

The [Zeisel lab gene browser](https://storage.googleapis.com/www_zeisellab/PE_web/pe_web_test_v1.html) shows where genes are active across placental cell types in the Admati/Skarbianskis preeclampsia single-cell dataset. Every team must look up at least three genes from its analysis.

## How to Use It

1. Open the browser and search for a gene by its symbol.
2. Record in your notebook which cell types express it and whether the pattern differs between preeclampsia and control samples.

## Good Uses

- Placing genes from your bulk analysis in broad placental cell types
- Supporting a hypothesis about which cells carry a pattern you found
- Comparing preeclampsia and control patterns as shown in the browser
- Choosing candidate genes for a proposed follow-up experiment

## Not Allowed

- Treating browser output as validation of a bulk result
- Using the browser as an answer key or a substitute for your own analysis
- Reporting screenshots as quantitative evidence
- Reprocessing the underlying raw data

## If the Browser Is Down

Use [`track01_marker_matrix.csv`](track01_marker_matrix.csv) together with the linked reference atlases in the [download guide](../README.md#4-linked-resources-not-downloaded). Overlap with a marker list alone does not show that your gene is expressed in that cell type.
